Our process

From grounds to jewellery

Reclaim

We collect spent coffee grounds from local cafés, before they become waste.

Bind

The grounds are dried and bound with a natural resin, slowly, by hand.

Shape

Each piece is shaped and cast individually — no two are exactly alike.

Finish

A final polish, gold detailing, and a quiet check before it leaves our hands.

Sustainability

Small, on purpose

We make in small batches, to order where we can, so nothing sits unworn in a warehouse. Reclaimed coffee grounds replace new raw material. We're not trying to be everywhere — just honest about what we make and why.

Reclaimed material

No new resource is mined or grown for a single earring.

Small batches

We make to order where we can, so little goes to waste.

Plastic-free packaging

What arrives at your door can be reused or composted.
